Start with one question

You do not need a perfect data environment to begin.

A useful first engagement can start with a single export. We assess what the records can answer today, what needs cleaning or restructuring, and what additional data would materially improve the answer.

“Where is the practice losing revenue?”

Start with billing, appointments, claims or patient activity.

“Which products actually drive the business?”

Start with a sales or stock export.

“Where are delays and costs accumulating?”

Start with orders, deliveries, routes or service records.
